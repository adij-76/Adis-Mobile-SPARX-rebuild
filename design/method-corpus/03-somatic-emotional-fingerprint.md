# Somatic & Emotional Fingerprint — Quick Reference

> Source: "DOCUMENT 3 – SOMATIC & EMOTIONAL FINGERPRINT MAP" (Adi Jaffe,
> Google Drive, captured 2026-07-06).

## 1. Why Somatic Awareness Matters

Emotions show up in the body **before** we consciously understand them.
Somatic signals help identify:
- The **type** of emotion (fear vs shame vs anger).
- When transference is happening (body overreacting to small stimuli).
- Early warning signs so the person can pause.

## 2. Common Emotion → Somatic Patterns

*(These are tendencies, not rules. The assistant should use them as gentle
hypotheses, not rigid truths.)*

**Anger / Rage** — heat in face · jaw clenching · tight chest · tension in arms/hands · urge to move fast or lash out

**Fear / Anxiety / Panic** — racing heart · shallow breathing · tight chest · nausea, knot in stomach · dizziness, light-headed · restless movement

**Shame / Humiliation** — wanting to hide or disappear · dropped head, avoiding eye contact · heat or flushing in face · heavy chest · collapse or sinking feeling

**Sadness / Grief** — heaviness in chest · lump in throat · tearfulness · slowed body movements · fatigue

**Powerlessness / Helplessness** — collapse in posture · sinking stomach · heaviness in limbs · sighing, low energy

**Disgust / Contempt** — nose wrinkling · pulling away physically · tight mouth, sneer

## 3. How to Ask About Somatic Activation

- "Where in your body did you feel this first?"
- "If you scan your body now, what do you notice?"
- "Did anything change in your breathing, chest, stomach, jaw, or face?"
- "What was your body trying to do — fight, flee, freeze, or collapse?"

## 4. How to Use Somatic Info

- Reflect it back as part of the **Activation** stage.
- Use it to help the user **notice early signals** in future triggers.
- Integrate into **pause protocols** ("When you feel your chest tighten,
  that's your cue to pause and take three breaths.")
