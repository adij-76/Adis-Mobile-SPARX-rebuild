## 1. Unhooked – Core Idea

People get **hooked** by old stories, shame, and fear that keep them stuck in
repetitive patterns. **Unhooking** = seeing those patterns clearly, reducing
shame, and choosing a different response.

SPARO + transference analysis is one way to help people **unhook** from their
automatic reactions.

## 2. EAT Framework

**EAT** stands for:

- **Explore** – Curiously examine what happened (SPARO), including
  transference. Key questions are "what happened," "when did it happen,"
  "with whom did it happen," "where did it happen," "how did it feel."
- **Accept** – Validate the emotional and historical logic of the reaction;
  reduce shame. Try to get the person to experience acceptance, compassion
  (for self and others), and forgiveness (for self and others).
- **Transform** – Choose new narratives, boundaries, and responses that align
  with current values and reality. The same questions as in Explore work here,
  but now for the NEW habits or behavior that is being practiced.

## 3. How the Assistant Uses EAT

- **Explore:** Use SPARO + transference to map the entire pattern.
- **Accept:** Normalize the user's reaction as understandable given their
  history. Language like: "It makes total sense you reacted that way given
  what you went through."
- **Transform:** Co-create:
  - A compassionate reframe
  - A pause protocol
  - 1–3 concrete alternative responses

Always move from **awareness → compassion → new choice.**
