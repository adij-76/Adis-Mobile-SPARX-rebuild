# Core Fears & Catastrophic Stories — Templates and Examples

> Source: "DOCUMENT 4 – CORE FEAR & CATASTROPHIC STORY TEMPLATES" (Adi Jaffe,
> Google Drive, captured 2026-07-06).

## 1. Definition

- **Core Fear:** The deep, often hidden fear beneath the immediate reaction.
- **Catastrophic Story:** The worst-case narrative the mind jumps to (usually
  shaped by past trauma, not present facts).

The goal is to **name** these clearly so they lose some of their unconscious power.

## 2. Common Core Fears

- "I will be abandoned and left alone."
- "I am unlovable / fundamentally flawed."
- "If I'm not perfect, I will be rejected."
- "I will end up helpless and dependent."
- "I will fail and lose everything."
- "No one will really be there for me."
- "If I don't control everything, things will fall apart."
- "If I express needs, I will be punished or shamed."
- "I will become like [family member] who collapsed/failed."

## 3. Catastrophic Story Templates

- "If this continues, they will leave me, I'll end up alone, and no one will ever love me again."
- "If I don't fix this, they'll fail in school, never get a job, and their life will fall apart."
- "If I make a mistake, I'll be exposed as a fraud, lose my job, and never recover."
- "If I don't constantly give, people will see me as selfish and abandon me."
- "If I let go of control, everything will collapse — financially, emotionally, relationally."

## 4. Questions to Elicit Core Fear

- "If this pattern continued for years, what's the worst you imagine could happen?"
- "What's the scariest possible outcome your mind jumps to?"
- "What do you fear this says about you, deep down?"
- "What do you fear this says about them, or their future?"

## 5. Differentiating Realistic Concern vs Catastrophic Story

The assistant should help the user separate:
- **Realistic concern:** Based on current facts and likely outcomes.
- **Catastrophic imagination:** Based on extreme, often past-driven narratives.

Example:
- Realistic: "My kid is behind in reading and needs consistent support."
- Catastrophic: "He will end up helpless, unemployable, and dependent forever."
